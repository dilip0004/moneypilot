==================================================
           MONEYPILOT - FUTURE ROADMAP
==================================================

This document outlines high-level strategic improvements to evolve 
MoneyPilot into an intelligent financial co-pilot.

--------------------------------------------------
1. INTELLIGENT AUTOMATION ("ZERO-INPUT" GOAL)
--------------------------------------------------
* BANK SMS PARSING: Auto-detect UPI/Bank SMS alerts to pre-fill 
  transactions (Critical for Indian market).
* OCR RECEIPT SCANNING: Extract amount, date, and merchant from 
  photos using on-device ML Kit.
* VOICE-DRIVEN ENTRY: Natural language processing for hands-free 
  logging (e.g., "Spent 500 on Fuel from HDFC").

--------------------------------------------------
2. ADVANCED VISUAL INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------
* PREDICTIVE INSIGHTS: Forecast end-of-month balance based on 
  current spending velocity.
* BUDGET HEATMAPS: Visual comparison of "Allocated Peace of Mind" 
  vs. actual usage.
* AI SUMMARIES: Human-readable insights (e.g., "You've saved 15% 
  more this week compared to your 3-month average").

--------------------------------------------------
3. PRIVACY & SECURITY REFINEMENT
--------------------------------------------------
* STEALTH MODE (MASKING): Global toggle to blur all currency 
  figures (₹1,234 -> ****) for public use.
* CLOUD-SYNC (ENCRYPTED): Auto-backup encrypted JSON to Google 
  Drive or OneDrive.
* PANIC PIN: Secondary PIN that displays a "Decoy" database with 
  fake low-balance data.

--------------------------------------------------
4. FINANCIAL LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------
* SUBSCRIPTION MANAGER: Track digital leakage (Netflix, iCloud) 
  with reminders 2 days before auto-debit.
* LOAN & DEBT TRACKER: Track EMI progress, interest vs. principal, 
  and payoff timelines.
* SHARED WALLETS: Multi-user sync for couples or small partners to 
  manage shared "Home" cash.

--------------------------------------------------
5. UI/UX "POWER-USER" POLISH
--------------------------------------------------
* GESTURE SHORTCUTS: Swipe-to-Action in Records list (e.g., Swipe 
  Right to Duplicate, Left to Delete).
* DYNAMIC WIDGETS: Home screen progress rings and category-specific 
  quick-add buttons.
* BATCH EDITING: Select multiple records to re-categorize or move 
  to a different account at once.

--------------------------------------------------
6. TECHNICAL DEBT & SCALING
--------------------------------------------------
* AUTO-MIGRATIONS: Robust database versioning to ensure zero data 
  loss during future updates.
* MULTI-CURRENCY ENGINE: Background conversion for international 
  travel or crypto assets.

==================================================
MoneyPilot: From Data Tracker to Intelligent Co-Pilot.
